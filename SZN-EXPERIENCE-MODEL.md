# The SZN experience + content model

Betty's decision, 2 Oct 2026: how every zodiac SZN works inside MY SZN. Use this whenever the product,
the sales copy or the content plan describes what members get. Companion to SZN-THEMES-Q4-2026.md
(what each SZN is about) and src/lib/doors.ts (when people join).

**The transformation is the product.** Never design MY SZN around the quantity of content members
receive, and never promise a fixed number of new pieces per month. Betty commits to TWO live
experiences per SZN, nothing more.

## Each zodiac SZN has

1. **1 live Transformation Workshop.** The main teaching + coaching experience for the SZN: the SZN
   theme, astrology, coaching, shadow work, practical exercises/reflection, Astro Tapping (EFT) and
   real-world action. This is where members understand what they're working on and begin the work.
   - Libra / Self-Love SZN: self-worth, receiving, standards, relationships, desire, confidence.
   - Scorpio / Bad B*tch SZN: shadow work, power, boundaries, conditioning, fear, money shadows,
     visibility, reclaiming parts of yourself.
   - Sagittarius / Big Dream SZN: expansion, manifestation, ambition, possibility, money, purpose,
     freedom, much bigger goals.
2. **1 live Community Circle.** Replaces any second formal workshop. Intimate, participatory,
   community-led and witchy, and NOT another webinar. Can include member check-ins, wins and
   breakthroughs, coaching/conversation, Astro Tapping where it fits, ritual, spellwork,
   manifestation practices, energy work, reflection, celebration, and working with what's coming up
   for members in real time. Purpose: belonging, embodiment, experiencing the transformation together.
3. **1 SZN Hypnosis.** Pre-recorded, one core hypnosis tied to the SZN theme, for members to return
   to all SZN for subconscious and identity-level change.
4. **Supporting audio guides, no promised quantity.** Short embodiment practices, guided
   visualisations, pep talks, reflection, energy work, nervous-system practices, short coaching
   audios, practices to use before a specific real-world action. Reuse existing vault resources
   where relevant; add new ones organically when they genuinely help.
5. **Ongoing community.** Prompts, conversations, wins, accountability, questions, members
   supporting each other.

## What each piece is for

- The personalised astrology/Human Design platform: members understand themselves and how the SZN
  applies to them.
- The workshop initiates the transformation.
- Astro Tapping works through what comes up.
- The hypnosis supports subconscious and identity change.
- The Circle creates embodiment, magic and belonging.
- Audio supports members between the bigger experiences.
- The community keeps the transformation alive in real life.
