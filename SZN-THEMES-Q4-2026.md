# SZN theme framework: Become Her Before 2027 (Q4 2026 chapter)

Betty's framework for the first MY SZN chapter, pasted 2 Oct 2026. This is the source of truth for
what changes each season while the MY SZN structure stays the same. The short lines (names, core
transformation, the question, the life-area lenses) are mirrored in code in `src/lib/szn-themes.ts`;
change them there too if they change here.

How the structure works: MY SZN runs in three-season chapters between cardinal ingresses (cardinal →
fixed → mutable). Doors open for a few days at the start of each season (`src/lib/doors.ts`), and
everyone inside lives the same current season together. Each SZN works across the member's whole
life; the theme changes the LENS through which we work on those areas.

---

## THE 90-DAY ARC

The final three months of 2026 follow one transformation:

**LOVE YOURSELF → HEAL THE SHADOW + CLAIM YOUR POWER → GO AFTER YOUR BIGGEST LIFE**

Each SZN works across the whole member's life: money, relationships, career, confidence, visibility, habits, wellbeing, lifestyle, purpose and goals.

The SZN theme changes the LENS through which we're working on those areas.

---

## 💗 LIBRA SZN: SELF-LOVE SZN

### Core transformation

**Love yourself enough to want more.**

Self-Love SZN is about changing the relationship you have with yourself before trying to change everything around you.

We're looking at self-worth, standards, receiving, pleasure, relationships, beauty, money, confidence, boundaries and desire.

This isn't surface-level "take a bath and buy yourself flowers" self-love.

We're asking harder questions.

Do you actually believe you deserve the life you say you want?

Where are you accepting less because asking for more feels uncomfortable?

Where are you abandoning yourself to keep relationships peaceful?

Can you receive money, love, praise, opportunities and support without immediately questioning whether you deserve them?

What would change if you genuinely valued yourself?

### Life areas

**Money:** Worth, receiving, pricing, asking for more and believing you're allowed to have LOTS of money.

**Love:** Standards, reciprocity, attachment, needs, communication and choosing relationships that actually feel good.

**Career:** Valuing your work, asking for what you deserve and choosing opportunities aligned with the life you want.

**Visibility:** Feeling worthy of being seen, heard, celebrated and recognised.

**Confidence:** Building self-trust rather than constantly looking outside yourself for validation.

**Lifestyle:** Pleasure, beauty, rest, joy and creating an environment you genuinely enjoy living in.

**Boundaries:** Recognising where saying yes to somebody else means saying no to yourself.

### Astrology + magic

Venus becomes an important archetype throughout the SZN.

We can work with Venus placements, receiving, attraction, worth, pleasure, glamour magic, beauty rituals, money magic and relationship patterns.

### The question of Self-Love SZN

**If I genuinely loved and valued myself, what would I stop accepting and what would I finally allow myself to have?**

### Destination

By the end of Self-Love SZN, she understands herself, her desires and her standards more deeply.

Now she's ready to confront whatever has been stopping her from living accordingly.

## 🦂 SCORPIO SZN: BAD B*TCH SZN

### Core transformation

**Heal the shadow. Reclaim your power.**

This is the deepest stage of the 90-day transformation.

Knowing you're worthy of more doesn't automatically mean you'll allow yourself to have it.

Bad B*tch SZN looks at WHY.

We're going into the shadow.

Fear.
Shame.
Conditioning.
People-pleasing.
Money wounds.
Visibility wounds.
Relationship patterns.
Control.
Jealousy.
Power.
Desire.
Sexuality.
Fear of judgement.
Fear of rejection.
Fear of success.
Fear of being too much.
Fear of becoming someone other people no longer understand.

We're looking at the versions of ourselves created through survival and asking whether they still belong in the life we're creating.

### This is where the deeper transformation happens

Bad B*tch SZN should contain significant shadow work, hypnosis, astrology, coaching, energy work, witchcraft and ritual.

We're not endlessly analysing our wounds.

We're identifying what needs healing, integrating, releasing or reclaiming before we ask ourselves to hold something bigger.

### Life areas

**Money:** Money shadows, scarcity, financial independence, receiving more, power around money and becoming harder to financially control.

**Love:** Boundaries, intimacy, attachment, desire, power dynamics and refusing to abandon yourself to keep someone else comfortable.

**Career:** Ambition, authority, asking for more and stopping yourself from shrinking professionally.

**Visibility:** Fear of judgement, criticism, rejection and being perceived.

**Confidence:** Owning the parts of yourself you've previously hidden or apologised for.

**Boundaries:** Saying no without writing a 700-word apology afterwards.

**Identity:** Letting versions of yourself die when they've become too small for where you're going.

### Feminist lens

This SZN directly examines the conditioning that teaches women to be agreeable, pleasant, accommodating, quiet, grateful and easy to deal with.

F*CK being easy to deal with.

We're becoming more difficult to manipulate, silence, underpay and control.

### Astrology + magic

Scorpio gives us shadow, transformation, death/rebirth, power, intimacy, taboo, desire and magic.

This can be one of the witchiest parts of MY SZN.

Spellwork, protection, energetic clearing, shadow rituals, power reclamation, hypnosis and transformation practices belong here.

### The question of Bad B*tch SZN

**What needs to change, heal or die so I can actually become the woman capable of holding the life I want?**

### Destination

By the end of Bad B*tch SZN, she has confronted more of what could sabotage her next chapter.

She's clearer on her boundaries.

She's reclaimed parts of herself.

She's becoming more powerful.

Now we're ready to ask:

How big do you actually want to go?

## 🏹 SAGITTARIUS SZN: BIG DREAM SZN

### Core transformation

**Go after your biggest life.**

Self-Love SZN taught us to want more.

Bad B*tch SZN helped us confront what was stopping us.

Now we're going BIG.

Big Dream SZN is about expansion, possibility, manifestation, courage, freedom, adventure, ambition and creating the vision for 2027.

This isn't simply making another vision board.

We're asking women to stop editing their dreams according to what feels realistic, socially acceptable or comfortable to everybody around them.

What do you actually want?

LOTS of money?

A completely different career?

A business?

Love?

Travel?

A book?

A baby?

A house somewhere ridiculous?

More freedom?

More visibility?

More pleasure?

An entirely different life?

Good.

Let's work with THAT.

### Life areas

**Money:** Bigger financial goals, wealth, expansion, earning capacity and what money could make possible.

**Love:** The relationship you genuinely want rather than the relationship you believe you should settle for.

**Career:** Bigger ambitions, opportunities, purpose, leadership and taking the scary opportunity.

**Visibility:** Reaching more people, sharing bigger ideas and allowing yourself to be known.

**Lifestyle:** Travel, freedom, experiences, adventure and designing a life that actually excites you.

**Confidence:** Developing the courage to pursue something before you have proof it'll work.

**Goals:** Turning enormous desires into actual decisions and actions.

### Manifestation

This is where manifestation becomes especially future-focused.

We're working with identity, belief, future self, possibility, timelines, hypnosis, visualisation, magic, practical action and the relationship between who someone is today and the reality she's creating.

### Astrology + magic

Sagittarius brings expansion, belief, possibility, adventure, wisdom and bigger horizons.

Magic can focus on expansion, abundance, manifestation, luck, opportunity and the vision for the year ahead.

The community experience could culminate in a huge **2027 Manifestation Party**.

### The question of Big Dream SZN

**If I stopped worrying about whether my dream was realistic, what would I actually choose?**

Then:

**What can I do NOW that makes that future more real?**

### Destination

She doesn't arrive on January 1st waiting to become a different person.

She's already changed.

She's clearer about what she wants.

She's raised her standards.

She's confronted shadows.

She's created boundaries.

She's reclaimed power.

She's allowed herself to dream bigger.

She's started making moves.

She enters 2027 ALREADY MOVING.
